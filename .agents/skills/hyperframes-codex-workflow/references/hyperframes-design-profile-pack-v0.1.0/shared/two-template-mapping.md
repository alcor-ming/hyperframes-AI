# Mapping One Profile to Two HyperFrames Templates

## Template A：口播增强 `talking_head`

### Role of HyperFrames

HyperFrames is an annotation and explanation layer. The person, original scene and spoken delivery remain the primary visual evidence.

### Shared limits

- At most one primary overlay focus at a time.
- At most one supporting information group.
- Text, panels and icons must remain outside the face protection zone.
- Existing caption bounds are permanent protected space.
- A Profile material may occupy negative space but must not turn the source footage into a dashboard.
- Macro information groups follow semantic sections, usually 6–12 seconds; this is not a requirement for continuous motion.
- When no new semantic information is needed, allow the original video to breathe without overlay.

### Ratio mapping

**16:9**

- Use the largest clean negative-space region beside the subject.
- Relationship lines may cross the center only when they do not cross face or captions.
- A middle sequence may appear only when the subject leaves enough unoccupied area.

**9:16**

- Default claim region: top safe zone.
- Default evidence region: below the subject or above existing captions.
- Do not create a permanent center HUD.
- Avoid the face and chest center; route relation marks along edges.

## Template B：纯 HyperFrames 展示 `pure_hyperframes`

### Role of HyperFrames

Typography, graphics, images and motion create the complete visual field. The Profile may use its full composition language, but hierarchy remains strict.

### Shared limits

- One primary message per scene.
- One dominant composition idea per scene.
- Decorative ambient motion must stay subordinate to the claim.
- Do not fill every region merely because no person is present.
- Build a readable static hero frame first, then animate toward it.
- The final stable state must remain readable long enough for the spoken phrase.

### Ratio mapping

**16:9**

- Supports asymmetric fields, lateral comparison, wide sequences and image/text counterpoint.
- Use depth or whitespace to keep a clear reading path.

**9:16**

- Prefer vertical chapters, stepped sequence and stacked focal transitions.
- Reduce simultaneous columns and preserve platform UI safe areas.
- Increase type size before adding more containers.

## Mode precedence

A Profile controls identity; the template controls occupation and density.

Examples:

- Optical Fluidity does not gain the right to place glass over a face.
- Kami Editorial does not force a full parchment background over talking-head footage.
- Monochrome Atelier does not reduce a necessary technical explanation to a single decorative word.
